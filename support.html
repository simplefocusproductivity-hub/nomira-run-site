Common questions

The app says it can't find GPS.

Step into the open and give it a few seconds — GPS rarely fixes indoors or in a stairwell. If it never fixes, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and confirm Nomira Run is allowed.

Tracking stops when my screen locks.

Grant location "Always" rather than "While Using the App". Distance and pace keep updating with the screen off only under "Always". On iOS, timer-driven voice cues may still wait until you unlock; distance-based coaching continues either way.

My Apple Watch heart rate isn't showing up.

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Nomira Run and make sure heart rate is enabled. Heart rate is optional — without it the app still coaches, it just can't ease your pace target when you're working hard.

Can I use a Bluetooth chest strap?

Yes. Choose Chest strap under Heart rate in Settings, and make sure the strap is awake and not connected to another app or watch at the time.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, completely. Nomira Run does all of its coaching on your device and needs no network at any point.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in Apple Health if you allowed it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nomira Run is a training tool, not a medical device. Its paces, effort targets and readiness scores are general fitness guidance. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme, and stop and seek help if you feel unwell while running.
