Common questions

The app says it can't find GPS.

Step into the open and give it a few seconds — GPS rarely fixes indoors or in a stairwell. If it never fixes, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and confirm Nomira Run is allowed.

Tracking stops when my screen locks.

It shouldn't. "While Using the App" is enough — you do not need to grant "Always". During a run you started, Nomira Run keeps measuring and the coach keeps talking with the screen locked and the phone in your pocket; you should see the blue location indicator in the status bar for the whole run. If it does stop, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services is on and Nomira Run is allowed, and email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Settings tab, choose Nomira Run Pro and tap Restore. It uses the Apple Account that bought it, including through Family Sharing. There is no Nomira account, so this is the only way back after a reinstall or a new phone.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

Settings → Your Data lists everything the app holds, exports it (runs, and GPX files for routes), and deletes it. Your history, export and delete-everything are free, always.

My Apple Watch heart rate isn't showing up.

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Nomira Run and make sure heart rate is enabled. Heart rate is optional — without it the app still coaches, it just can't ease your pace target when you're working hard.

Can I use a Bluetooth chest strap?

Yes. Choose Chest strap under Heart rate in Settings, and make sure the strap is awake and not connected to another app or watch at the time.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, completely. Nomira Run does all of its coaching on your device and needs no network at any point.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in Apple Health if you allowed it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nomira Run is a training tool, not a medical device. Its paces, effort targets and readiness suggestions are general fitness guidance. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme, and stop and seek help if you feel unwell while running.
