Privacy Policy — Nomira Run

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: Nomira Run does not have accounts, does not have servers, and does not send your data anywhere. Everything the app records — your runs, your routes, your heart rate — is stored on your iPhone and, if you choose, in Apple Health. We cannot see any of it.

Who we are

Nomira Run is an independent iOS app. Questions about this policy or your data: brian.letuan@gmail.com.

What the app accesses, and why

Every one of these is optional in the sense that iOS asks you first, and you can refuse or revoke any of them in Settings → Privacy & Security. Refusing location means the app cannot measure a run; refusing heart rate simply turns off effort-aware coaching.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your data on any server. The app has no backend.
  • We do not have user accounts, and never ask you to create one.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not ask for permission to, because we have nothing to track you with.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. There is no third party to share it with.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Nomira Run reads heart rate (and, for the readiness suggestion, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep) from Apple Health and writes your completed runs back to it. That exchange happens entirely on your device, through Apple's HealthKit framework. Health data is never transmitted off the device by this app, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can revoke either permission at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you are in control of all of it. In the app, Settings → Your Data exports everything (runs, and GPX files for routes) and deletes everything in two taps. Deleting the app also removes your settings and run history from your device. Your purchase is kept by Apple, not by us, and comes back with Restore. Runs already written to Apple Health belong to Health, not to this app — delete those in the Health app if you want them gone.

Children

Nomira Run is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask for your consent first.
